Project case study

Storii by ITC Hotels, Jaisalmer

Overview

We are proud to have successfully delivered the complete manufacturing and installation of bespoke luxury resort tents for Storii by ITC Hotels, Jaisalmer. This prestigious project marks a significant milestone in our journey, reinforcing our position as a trusted tent manufacturer and approved vendor for ITC Hotels. Our successful execution reflects our commitment to delivering premium-quality hospitality structures that meet the highest standards of luxury, durability and performance.

Situated amidst the majestic desert landscape of Jaisalmer, the project required a solution that would meet the premium hospitality standards of the Storii brand while withstanding extreme climatic conditions. Through innovative design, precision engineering and meticulous execution, we delivered a collection of luxury tents that offer guests an immersive yet highly comfortable desert experience.

The Challenge

Jaisalmer's harsh desert climate presents unique construction challenges. The client required accommodation structures that could endure demanding conditions without compromising the elegance and sophistication expected from a premium hospitality destination.

  • Extreme temperature variations between day and night
  • High wind loads and sand exposure
  • The need for year-round guest comfort
  • Maintaining a luxury aesthetic in a remote environment

Our Solution

Our team engineered and installed a custom fleet of luxury glamping tents designed to deliver both performance and prestige.

Aesthetic Excellence

Elegant architectural detailing and premium interior finishes were carefully aligned with Storii's distinctive design philosophy, complementing the natural beauty of the desert.

Advanced Engineering

High-tensile, weather-resistant fabric systems and robust framing were engineered for long-term durability, stability and desert performance.

Exceptional Guest Comfort

Advanced insulation and superior structural integrity enable efficient climate control and year-round comfort while maintaining a connection to nature.

Outcome

This project stands as a testament to our ability to deliver luxury hospitality structures in demanding environments. By combining innovative engineering with premium design, we created a hospitality solution that offers guests the comfort of a luxury resort while embracing the authenticity and beauty of the desert landscape.

“This project reflects our commitment to redefining luxury outdoor hospitality, demonstrating that exceptional comfort, architectural elegance, and remote natural settings can coexist in perfect harmony.”

The successful completion further reinforces our position as a trusted partner for luxury resorts, hospitality brands and glamping destinations seeking distinctive, high-performance accommodation solutions.
