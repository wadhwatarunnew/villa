Due Care

Our products are made with heavy-duty polyester ripstop canvas exteriors. Keep the product in conditions where it can dry promptly after rainfall to prevent the build-up of mildew and mould. Canvas and linings need to be cleaned with water at the end of each season and folded away completely dry.

The cotton linings will not shrink but must be cleaned or hand-washed in cold water to prevent dyes from fading. Dyes will fade over time from prolonged sun exposure, creating a weathered look. Our fabric is treated with a flame-retardant chemical; because this property reduces over time, we recommend applying flame-retardant chemicals to the tent fabric periodically.
